About Me

Designing with purpose

Hi, I'm Vijeta — a UI/UX designer who enjoys turning complex problems into simple, intuitive, and engaging digital experiences.

I've worked on projects across different areas, including productivity tools, work-management experiences, and websites, exploring everything from user flows and wireframes to visual design and prototyping.

For me, good design is not just about making something look good — it's about understanding users, solving the right problems, and creating experiences that feel natural and easy to use.

Every project has been a chance to learn, experiment, and improve my design skills. I'm curious by nature, enjoy exploring new ideas, and believe there's always something new to learn through design.
